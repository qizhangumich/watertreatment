Partnership

Where we can fit into your project.

The opportunities below are starting points for technical and commercial discussion. Scope, availability and responsibilities are confirmed per project.

  1. 01

    OEM and technology supply

    Membrane or ultrafiltration scopes for integrators and equipment builders.

    To start: Share process basis, target flow and interface limits.

  2. 02

    EPC work packages

    Defined equipment packages inside a larger contractor-led project.

    To start: Share package boundary, specification and programme.

  3. 03

    Replacement and retrofit

    Membrane replacement planning, or connecting no-aeration UF to existing treatment units.

    To start: Share current system data, operating history and constraints.

  4. 04

    Remote and field facilities

    Containerized desalination, reuse or wastewater units for off-grid and remote operations.

    To start: Share water source, demand, power and access conditions.

  5. 05

    Project-specific solutions

    Model selection or multi-unit concepts for a defined site.

    To start: Share feedwater analysis and treated-water target.

What makes a useful first enquiry

  1. Location and water source
  2. Feedwater analysis (TDS, COD, SS, temperature)
  3. Required daily flow and treated-water standard
  4. Available power, area and access route
  5. Project stage, target date and your role in the project
