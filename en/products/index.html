How no-aeration PTFE filtration works

Conventional submerged membranes use blower air to shake the fibers. Here, a mechanical drive moves the module back and forth. The resulting relative motion between fiber and mixed liquor scrubs the surface and helps prevent clogging, with lower energy use and noise than air scouring.

  • Dry in-place cleaning and dry storage of PTFE membranes
  • Design flux of 10–30 LMH (AquaUltra)
  • Connects to existing biological or chemical-precipitation units
  • Treated-water target of SDI < 5 (AquaUltra)
