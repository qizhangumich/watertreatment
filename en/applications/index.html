Applications

Start with the water problem.

Each application below links the operating challenge to a treatment route and to products documented in the source datasheets.

01

Seawater desalination

The problem
Coastal cities, industries and islands need dependable fresh water where conventional sources are scarce, and energy is usually the largest operating concern.
Technology
Multimedia pretreatment, seawater reverse osmosis and energy recovery. Large plants use SW 400 and SW 440 class membrane elements.
Relevant products
LG Chem SW series elements (reference grades); AquaSea™ C50 to C1000E
Project fit
Membrane supply or replacement in large SWRO plants; 50–1,000 m³/day packages for feedwater of 20,000–45,000 mg/L TDS.
02

Municipal and decentralized wastewater

The problem
Small communities and facilities often sit beyond sewer networks and lack the land or staff for a conventional treatment works.
Technology
Anoxic and aerobic stages with hydrophilic denitrification media, followed by no-aeration PTFE MBR.
Relevant products
AquaBiomax™ 100, 300 and 500
Project fit
Towns, schools, hospitals, emergency facilities, highway service areas, tourist sites and industrial parks, at 100–500 m³/day per unit.
03

Water reuse and reclaimed water

The problem
Industrial sites face water limits and discharge pressure while treated effluent still holds recoverable value.
Technology
No-aeration PTFE MBR/MCR feeds reverse osmosis directly through a continuous-flow arrangement, with no intermediate tank.
Relevant products
AquaReclaim™ 500 and 1000
Project fit
Textile dyeing, mine water, recirculating cooling water, steel and coal-chemical reuse, at 500–1,000 m³/day per unit.
04

Ultrafiltration and membrane retrofit

The problem
Difficult, high-solids streams can foul conventional membranes, and air scouring adds energy demand and noise.
Technology
A mechanical drive reciprocates the membrane module so inertia moves the fibers and limits fouling, replacing air scour.
Relevant products
AquaUltra™ 1000 and 3000
Project fit
Connection to existing biological or chemical-precipitation units; steel, paper, textile, municipal, pharmaceutical and food-service streams.
05

Oilfield and energy facilities

The problem
Remote and offshore assets depend on costly delivered water, while power and process plants need a secure make-up source.
Technology
Containerized SWRO for remote supply; large-format SWRO elements for power and petrochemical desalination plants.
Relevant products
AquaSea™ series; SW 400 and SW 440 grades
Project fit
Datasheets list drilling platforms and factory emergency supply as AquaSea applications. References include power, nuclear and petrochemical desalination plants.
06

Other industrial and site uses

The problem
Temporary or isolated operations need treatment capacity without a permanent plant.
Technology
Select the package by feedwater: seawater RO, dual-membrane reuse, MBR or ultrafiltration.
Relevant products
AquaSea™, AquaReclaim™, AquaBiomax™, AquaUltra™
Project fit
Construction sites, ocean vessels, island communities, hotels and resorts, electronics, chemicals, paper and pharmaceuticals.
