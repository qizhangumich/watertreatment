Project references

A reference list you can check line by line.

Forty-seven listed seawater desalination references across ten countries, from 2,000 m³/day facilities to plants above 600,000 m³/day.

How to read these references

These entries come from LG Chem’s 2025 desalination reference list and relate to LG Chem seawater RO membranes. The plants were delivered and operated by their owners, customers and contractors. They are not presented as projects executed by the publisher of this website.
