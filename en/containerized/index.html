Containerized systems

Treatment capacity that travels to the demand.

Twelve international models cover seawater desalination, reuse, wastewater and ultrafiltration, from 50 to 3,000 m³/day.

Containerized systems

Why modular

  • Decentralized

    Place treatment near islands, oilfields, camps, communities, industrial sites, construction projects or temporary facilities.

  • Compact

    Process stages are integrated inside the enclosure. Footprints from about 14.1 m² per unit are listed below.

  • Prefabricated

    Equipment arrives factory-integrated, which can reduce on-site civil and installation scope. Actual programme depends on the site.

  • Scalable

    Choose the model that matches demand. Multi-unit and phased layouts need project-specific engineering confirmation.

Containerized systems

Installation considerations

  • Transport envelope

    AquaSea models use 20 ft and 40 ft dimensions. Several other models are 3,500 mm high, above the 2,896 mm high-cube height, so route and shipping method need early review.

  • Foundations

    Design for operating weight, not empty weight. Operating weights reach 80 t on AquaBiomax-500.

  • Site interfaces

    Confirm intake, outfall or brine discharge, sludge handling, storage, power supply and access.

  • Water standard

    Confirm the required standard and any post-treatment before selection. Datasheet values do not establish regulatory compliance.
