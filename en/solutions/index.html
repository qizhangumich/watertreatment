Solutions

Two models. One clear division of responsibility.

Large projects need a dependable specialist in the supply chain. Decentralized sites need a complete unit. The two models answer different buying decisions.

Model A: Large-project supplyModel B: Containerized systems
Typical buyerPlant owners, EPC contractors, O&M operators, procurement teamsSite operators, industrial groups, communities, energy and remote-facility operators
What is suppliedRO membrane elements, UF and MBR packages, process equipment scopesIntegrated treatment module with process, controls and monitoring
ScaleListed references from 2,000 to 636,300 m³/day50 to 3,000 m³/day per model
Why buyQualified components, replacement supply, defined interfacesCompact footprint, factory integration, placement near demand
Commercial routePackage or component purchase within a wider contractDirect equipment purchase, with site scope agreed separately
Overall deliveryRemains with the owner or EPC contractorCivil works, intake, outfall and permits are agreed per project
